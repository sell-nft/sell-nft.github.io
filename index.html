Fee sources and calculation method

Platform rates were checked on October 9, 2026 against Blur's marketplace homepage (0%), OpenSea Help Center's “What fees do I pay on OpenSea?” (standard secondary sales: 1%), and Rarible's September 1, 2026 FAQ (direct Ethereum listings: 2%). OpenSea private listings typically carry a 0% platform fee. Primary drops and purchases of aggregated listings use separate fee schedules.

Net proceeds = sale price * (1 - platform fee / 100 - creator royalty / 100) - seller-paid gas. The same sale price, royalty and gas entries apply to each row so the platform fee difference stays visible. Enter each venue's specific costs in the dashboard to compare different offers. How to sell an NFT,
from wallet to payout

Have your NFT in the selling wallet, confirm its network, and leave enough ETH for any approvals or transactions.

  1. 1

    Check the collection and recent sales

    Match the NFT's contract address and token ID. Compare completed sales for similar traits, the collection floor and active offers. Asking prices show competition; completed sales show what buyers have paid.

  2. 2

    Choose a price or an existing offer

    Set a fixed price and an expiry if you can wait for a buyer. For a faster sale, compare active bids. Use the net payout after all selling costs to choose between them.

  3. 3

    Review, sign and confirm settlement

    Check the asset, currency, price, royalty and expiry in the wallet request. Approve the marketplace contract when needed, then sign. After the sale confirms on-chain, check the payment in your wallet and cancel other active listings.

Which NFT selling method fits your timing?

Fixed-price listing

Choose the price a buyer can pay immediately. Use recent comparable sales to set a competitive ask, and give the listing an expiry so you can revisit the price.

Timing: depends on a buyer matching your ask.

Accept an offer

Sell into an existing item or collection bid. Check that it covers your NFT, the payment token is correct, and the proceeds still meet your target after seller-paid gas.

Timing: settlement follows transaction confirmation.

Timed auction

Where supported, invite competing bids over a defined period. A reserve price sets the minimum you will accept. Check settlement rules and seller fees for that auction.

Timing: auction duration plus settlement.

How to sell NFTs safely

  • Check the signature, not just the screen. Match the contract, token ID, payment amount and recipient to the sale you intended.
  • Keep recovery phrases private. Use your wallet to approve a sale. Reject requests to type a seed phrase into a website or chat.
  • Read approval permissions. Check whether an approval covers one token or a collection, and revoke permissions you no longer need.
  • Verify payment on-chain. Confirm funds in your wallet before treating a sale as settled. Ignore requests to send an “activation” payment to a buyer.

Sell NFT FAQ

Fees, pricing and payment questions to settle before you list.

Where is the cheapest place to sell an NFT?

For the Ethereum marketplace fees compared here, Blur charges 0%, OpenSea normally charges 1%, and Rarible charges 2% on direct listings. Compare the final proceeds after collection royalties and gas. The highest available buyer offer can matter more than the lowest fee.

Can I sell an NFT without paying gas upfront?

An off-chain listing signature can be free after the required token approval is in place. Initial approvals, accepting offers and on-chain cancellations can cost gas. On OpenSea, the buyer pays transaction gas when purchasing a fixed-price listing; the seller pays gas when accepting an offer.

How do I choose a price for my NFT?

Start with recent completed sales for the same collection and similar traits. Compare the current floor and funded offers, then calculate the price needed to meet your target payout after fees. For a faster sale, evaluate bids already available for your token.

How long does it take to sell an NFT?

A fixed-price listing waits for a buyer, which can take minutes, days or longer. Accepting a valid funded offer moves directly to the transaction stage. Settlement then depends on network confirmation, while an auction also includes its bidding period.

What is the difference between platform fees and royalties?

A platform fee pays the marketplace for the sale. A creator royalty directs part of the sale price to the collection's creator. Each is calculated separately from the price. Network gas pays for blockchain transactions and is an additional cost.

Do I receive ETH or WETH when my NFT sells?

The listing or offer specifies the payment token. An ETH-denominated fixed-price purchase pays in ETH, while Ethereum offers often use WETH, the wrapped form of ETH. Check the token before accepting. Unwrapping WETH to ETH is a separate transaction with a gas cost.

Can I sell the same NFT on several marketplaces?

You can create listings on multiple compatible marketplaces while holding the NFT. Once it sells, review and cancel any remaining active orders. An unexpired signed order may become fillable again if the NFT returns to your wallet and its approval remains active.
